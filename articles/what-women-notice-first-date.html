What I Notice About a Man on a First Date

By Alina · A personal perspective

First dates can feel a little awkward because you both know you're making a first impression. Personally, I don't really pay attention to expensive clothes or perfect lines. I notice the smaller things first.

1. Whether he seems comfortable being himself

For me, confidence doesn't mean being the loudest person in the room. I like when a guy seems comfortable with himself and doesn't spend the whole date trying to impress me. It makes the conversation feel much easier.

2. The basic details

I definitely notice the basics: clean clothes, grooming, fresh breath and whether he looks like he made a little effort. It doesn't have to be expensive at all. I just like seeing that he cared about the date.

3. How he treats people around us

I always pay attention to how a guy talks to waiters, bartenders or anyone else around us. I also notice how he reacts when something doesn't go exactly as planned. Little moments like that tell me a lot.

4. Whether the conversation goes both ways

I don't enjoy a date that feels like an interview, but I don't want to listen to one person talk about himself all night either. I like when he asks questions, listens to my answers and lets the conversation happen naturally.

5. How I feel around him

This one is hard to explain, but it matters the most to me. Do I feel comfortable? Am I laughing without forcing it? Do I actually want to stay a little longer? Sometimes that feeling tells me more than anything else on the date.

These are just the things I tend to notice. Every woman is different, so someone else might pay attention to completely different things.
