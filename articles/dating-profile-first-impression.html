What I Notice First on a Man's Dating Profile

By Alina · A personal perspective

When I open a dating profile, I mainly want to get a quick sense of who the guy actually is. I don't expect a perfect profile. I just want it to feel real.

A clear first photo

If the first photo is a group picture and I have to figure out which guy the profile belongs to, that's already not ideal for me. I prefer a recent, clear photo where I can actually see his face. It doesn't need heavy editing.

Some variety

Five almost identical selfies don't tell me very much. I like seeing a few different photos because they give me a better idea of his personality, interests and everyday life.

A bio that gives me something to respond to

I prefer a short bio with one or two real details over a list of words like 'funny, ambitious, adventurous.' Mentioning something specific makes it much easier for me to start a conversation.

The overall feeling

More than anything, I notice the overall vibe. Does he seem friendly? Does the profile feel negative? Is he trying too hard to look impressive? Personally, I'm much more curious about someone who seems comfortable being himself.
