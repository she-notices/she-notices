What Should a Guy Wear on a First Date?

By Alina · A personal perspective

If you asked me what a man should wear on a first date, I wouldn't start with a specific brand or expensive outfit. I'd start with context.

Dress for where you're actually going

A coffee date and a nice dinner don't call for the same outfit. Looking comfortable in the setting is usually better than being dramatically overdressed.

Fit matters more than labels

Simple clothes that fit well can look much better than expensive pieces that don't. Clean shoes and clothes that look cared for also make a difference.

Keep some personality

I wouldn't want someone to dress like a completely different person just for a date. A first date should give both people a glimpse of the real person.

Don't forget grooming

Hair, nails, facial hair and fragrance are part of the overall impression. With fragrance especially, I prefer subtle over overwhelming.

The goal isn't to look perfect. It's to look like the best, most comfortable version of yourself.
