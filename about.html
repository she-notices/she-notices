About

Hi, I'm Alina.

She Notices is where I share my personal perspective on dating, attraction, first impressions and men's style.

I don't believe one woman can tell you what every woman thinks. That's why everything here is written as a perspective rather than a rulebook. I talk about the things I notice, the choices that catch my attention, and the dating questions that are more interesting when you hear an answer from the other side.

Sometimes that means dating profiles. Sometimes it's what someone wears, how a conversation feels, or whether a fragrance actually leaves an impression. The goal is simple: thoughtful, useful and easy-to-read answers without pretending dating is a formula.

Think of She Notices as a conversation, not a set of rules.
